## What this skill does

A fast kill-switch for new ideas, run before real time gets spent — a new client offer, an internal automation, a pricing change, a positioning angle. Five distinct critical perspectives, one after another, each looking for something the others would miss. Ends in one verdict, not a vague "seems fine."

Use this before committing to something new, not after it's already half-built — the whole point is catching a bad idea cheap.

## The five personas

Run each in turn, actually adopting the lens rather than blending them into one generic critique:

1. **The Contrarian** — assumes the idea is wrong and argues why. What's the strongest case against this? Who would say no, and what's their real reason?
2. **The Expansionist** — assumes the idea is right and pushes it further. If this works, what's the 10x version? Is the current scope too small to matter?
3. **The Logician** — checks the internal reasoning, not the vibe. Does the math actually work? Does step 2 really follow from step 1? Where's the hidden assumption doing all the load-bearing work?
4. **The Researcher** — asks what's already known. Has someone tried this and failed? Is there existing data (past projects, `decisions/log.md`, notes in `references/`) that already answers part of this?
5. **The Buyer** — sits in the actual customer's seat. Would a real prospect pay for this, at this price, today? What would make them say no that has nothing to do with the idea's quality (timing, trust, budget cycle)?

## Output

After all five: one verdict — **go**, **reshape** (name exactly what changes), or **kill** (name exactly why) — plus the single cheapest, fastest way to find out if the idea actually holds up, doable in the next 48 hours (a single client conversation, a $50 test, a one-page draft sent to one person — not a full build).

## Rules

- Don't soften the personas into agreement. If two of them contradict each other, say so — that tension is often the most useful part of the output.
- This is a filter, not a planning session. If the verdict is "go" or "reshape," the actual scoping happens elsewhere (`/grill-me` or a normal build).
- Keep it to the five lenses plus the verdict — don't pad with extra frameworks.
